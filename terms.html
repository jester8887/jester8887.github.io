Important Terms for Custom Mix Projects

Please read before ordering!

1

Deposits & Cancellations

A deposit is required to reserve production time. Cancellations within 7 days of payment receive a 50% deposit refund. After 7 days, deposits are nonrefundable.

2

Final Payment & Previews

The remaining balance is due within 24 hours of first draft delivery. Previews expire and may disappear from message threads. Once payment is received, expired previews can be resent. Revisions begin only after full payment.

3

Included Revisions

Standard orders include 2 revisions for adjustments to transitions, song excerpts, and mix length. Revisions are for refining existing work, not rebuilding or substantially restructuring it. Extensive requests may count as both revisions. Adding/replacing songs, new sound effects, and extending the mix are not included and may incur additional fees.

4

Requesting Revisions

Each revision must be submitted as one consolidated message. Once I acknowledge your requests, work is considered underway. Additional requests after that point may incur fees.

5

Revision Deadlines

Please submit feedback within 5 days of each draft. After 5 days, your project moves to the back of my queue. After 14 days, additional fees may apply.

6

Revision Turnaround

Standard revisions are generally completed within 7 days; rush-order revisions within 3 days. Late feedback may delay completion.

7

Song Timestamps

Timestamps are preferred but optional. Without them, revisions are limited to 1 excerpt change per minute of the final mix. An excerpt change means selecting a different portion of an existing song.

8

Mix Length & Pricing

Your requested timestamps must fit the quoted mix length. If they exceed it, additional time will be added to your balance. Removing songs or shortening the mix does not reduce the quoted price.

9

Custom Features & Exclusivity

Unless agreed upon when ordering, mixes do not include overlapping songs, custom remixing, added beats/layers, or exclusivity. These options are generally available upon request. Adding custom features after first draft delivery incurs an additional fee.

10

Production & Delivery

First drafts are delivered within 2 weeks of deposit payment. Rush orders are often available for an additional fee based on urgency and mix length. Completed files are delivered via Google Drive download link.
